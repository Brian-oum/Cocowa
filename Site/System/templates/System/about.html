Dedicated To Passenger Welfare.

The Counties Commuter Welfare Association (COCOWA) is a dedicated organization committed to enhancing the travel experience across road, rail, water, and air. We advocate for policies that minimize safety risks and ensure equitable and dignified treatment for all travelers.

Our Mission

To increase passenger safety by 90% by 2030 through diligent monitoring, impactful partnerships, and initiatives that promote safety awareness, accessibility, and equitable travel conditions, contributing directly to Kenya's Vision 2030 and SDG 11 goals.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in passenger safety advocacy, setting standards that foster safe, inclusive, and sustainable travel across all modes of transport.

Our Values

Transparency - Promoting open, accessible communication and accountability in all actions to build stakeholder trust.
Accountability - Demonstrating responsibility for all initiatives and ensuring they align with the objectives of SDG 11 and Vision 2030.
Sincerity - Encouraging trust and goodwill through honest and respectful interactions with passengers and partners.
